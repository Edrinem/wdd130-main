Pearl Rapids Rafting

Our mission is to give every guest a thrilling, safe, and unforgettable day on the river, guided by people who love the water as much as you will.

History

Pearl Rapids Rafting began with two friends, one worn-out raft, and a big idea: share the power of the river with everyone. Over the years we have grown into a team of certified guides who have taken thousands of guests safely through some of the best rapids in the region.
