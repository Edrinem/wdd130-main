Rafting.co

Rafting.co started in 2012 with two friends, one patched-up raft, and a simple belief that the best way to see a river is from the middle of it. What began as weekend trips for neighbors grew quickly by word of mouth. Within three years we had a team of certified guides, a fleet of new rafts, and a reputation for putting safety first and fun always. Today we have welcomed more than 20,000 guests, from nervous first-timers to seasoned paddlers. Every trip still begins the way our first one did: with a safety talk, a big smile, and a shout of "Paddle!"
